An **adjacency list** records a graph by giving each vertex a list of its neighbors, the vertices joined to it by one edge. In an undirected graph the edge A–B appears twice, in A's list and in B's, so all the lists together hold 2E entries.

It takes O(V + E) memory and lets [[en/concepts/breadth-first-search|BFS]] read a vertex's neighbors in time proportional to how many it has. On a [[en/concepts/grid-graph|grid]] I don't need to store the lists because neighbors come from coordinates; in an [[en/concepts/implicit-graph|implicit graph]], neighbors are generated by a rule.
