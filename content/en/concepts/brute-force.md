**Brute force** tries every possibility, for example comparing every pair of elements (n(n−1)/2 pairs, O(n²)). It is a good starting point: it shows you understand the problem and gives you a correct version to check against when testing faster approaches. In Contains Duplicate, brute force on 10,000 numbers takes 38 ms, while 100,000 numbers would take several seconds.
