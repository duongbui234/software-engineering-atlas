An **implicit graph** comes with no [[en/concepts/adjacency-list|adjacency list]]. I only know the rule: in Word Ladder, two words are neighbors if they differ in exactly one letter and the new word is in the list. BFS runs as usual; only the "neighbors of x" function has to generate its answer.

How I generate neighbors decides the cost. Comparing x with every word costs N comparisons per vertex. Grouping words into pattern buckets (`c*rd`), or trying each single-letter change and looking it up in a set, is much faster when the list is large. Chess boards, combination locks and the states of a game are implicit graphs too.
